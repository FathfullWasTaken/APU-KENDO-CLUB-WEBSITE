About APU Kendo Club

  • APU Kendo Club, based at Ritsumeikan Asia Pacific University in Japan, is a student-led club where more than half the members are international students from 19 countries. Through the spirit of koukenchiai (交剣知愛) — building mutual understanding by crossing swords — the club brings Japanese and international students together to train, compete, and experience Japanese martial arts firsthand.
  • History: Founded in 2018, it trained many students, including members who reached the national team for the 2025 ASEAN Kendo Tournament. You can view updates on their Facebook Page
